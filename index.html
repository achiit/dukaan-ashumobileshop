Since 2016

Started as a repair counter, grown into a full shop

My father ran a small radio and cassette repair stall on this very street back in the nineties. When mobiles reached Fatehpur, I started fixing them alongside him, and opened Ashu Mobile Shop under my own name in 2016.

We still open the box and set up every phone in front of the customer before it leaves the counter. If a setting or app confuses you, come back anytime and we will sort it out, no charge.

  • Genuine bill and warranty on every phone
  • Free tempered glass fitted before delivery
  • Old phone exchange and EMI available
  • WhatsApp support 24 hours a day
